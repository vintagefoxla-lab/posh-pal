## Best Practices

1. **Personalize every message** — reference specific content they've created
2. **Keep it conversational** — avoid corporate jargon
3. **Lead with value** — make it clear what they get before what they give
4. **Make it easy** — send calendar links, not "let me know when you're free"
5. **Track everything** — log outreach in the influencer database
6. **Follow FTC guidelines** — always require #ad or #sponsored disclosure
