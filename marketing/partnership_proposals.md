# Posh Pal — Influencer Partnership Tiers

**Last Updated:** 2026-06-17

## Tier 1 — Posh Pal Ambassador (Paid Sponsorship)
**Target:** Macro-influencers (100K–500K followers on any platform)
**Value:** $500–$2,000/month + Free Pro Lifetime

### Deliverables
- **1 dedicated video/reel/tiktok per month** featuring Posh Pal walkthrough
- **2–3 social posts/stories per month** mentioning Posh Pal
- **Unique referral link** with custom discount code for followers (e.g., `POSHPAL_AMBASSADOR_20`)
- **1 testimonial/quote** for landing page and social proof
- **Monthly check-in call** with Posh Pal marketing team

### Influencer Benefits
- $500–$2,000 monthly cash compensation (negotiable based on reach)
- **Lifetime Pro subscription** (value: $180/year)
- Early access to all new features
- Co-branded content opportunities
- Featured on Posh Pal website and social channels
- Dedicated onboarding support

### Posh Pal Benefits
- 15% affiliate commission on all sales through their referral link
- Performance bonus: $100 extra for every 50 subscribers acquired in a month
- First access to beta features to create exclusive content

### Ideal Fit
- Content creators who already use Poshmark or reselling tools
- Creators whose audience is at least 40% resellers or aspiring sellers
- High engagement rate (>4%) preferred

## Tier 2 — Posh Pal Partner (Affiliate Commission)
**Target:** Mid-tier influencers (50K–200K followers)
**Value:** 20% recurring commission + Free Pro Access

### Deliverables
- **2 organic mentions per month** (stories, posts, or video mentions)
- **Affiliate link in bio** (Linktree/Bio.site/ShopMy)
- **1 tool demonstration/review** within 60 days of joining
- **Quarterly feedback session** with Posh Pal team (15 min)

### Influencer Benefits
- **20% recurring commission** on all Pro subscription sales (avg. $3/month per referral)
- **Free Pro access** (value: $180/year)
- Early access to new features
- Affiliate dashboard to track real-time earnings
- Featured in Posh Pal partner directory

### Posh Pal Benefits
- Consistent organic mentions across the creator's content cycle
- Authentic recommendations from trusted voices
- Low fixed cost — performance-based compensation

### Ideal Fit
- Influencers who frequently recommend tools and resources to their audience
- Poshmark-focused content creators with engaged communities
- Creators who have done affiliate partnerships before

---

## Tier 3 — Posh Pal Creator (Free Pro Access)
**Target:** Micro-influencers (10K–80K followers)
**Value:** Free Pro subscription (value: $15/month or $180/year)

### Deliverables
- **1 social mention/story per month** sharing their experience with Posh Pal
- **Optional:** Participate in a Posh Pal case study or testimonial
- Provide feedback on features and user experience

### Influencer Benefits
- **Full Pro subscription free** (unlimited AI listings, analytics, sharing bot, cross-listing)
- No financial commitment required
- Access to all new features as they launch
- Direct line to Posh Pal product team for feedback

### Posh Pal Benefits
- Build pipeline of future paid ambassadors
- Gather authentic product testimonials
- Increase brand awareness in niche communities
- Product feedback from real power users

### Ideal Fit
- Niche fashion/resale content creators
- Active Poshmark sellers with a following
- Creators new to brand partnerships but with high community trust

## Conversion Path

```
Tier 3 (Free Pro)  ──►  Tier 2 (Affiliate)  ──►  Tier 1 (Paid Sponsorship)
     (3 months)              (6 months)             (ongoing)
```

- After 3 months on Tier 3, creators who show strong engagement are invited to Tier 2
- After 6 months of consistent referrals on Tier 2, creators are invited to apply for Tier 1
- All tiers include direct communication with the Posh Pal team

---

## Tracking & Attribution

- **Unique referral codes:** Each influencer gets a unique discount code (e.g., `POSHPAL_INFLUENCER_20`)
- **Affiliate dashboard:** Real-time tracking of clicks, signups, and commissions
- **UTM parameters:** All influencer links use branded UTM parameters for analytics
- **Monthly reporting:** Automated report sent to each partner showing their performance

---

## Legal & Compliance

- All partners must disclose partnership per FTC guidelines (#ad, #sponsored)
- Standard influencer agreement contract with 30-day cancellation clause
- Payment terms: Net-15 for Tier 1, Net-30 commission payouts for Tier 2
- Non-exclusive — influencers can promote competing tools but must give Posh Pal priority placement
